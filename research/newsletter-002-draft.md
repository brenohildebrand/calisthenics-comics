**Move of the week: Pull-Up (Level 4, Brawler)**
Overhand grip, pull your chest toward the bar until your chin is over it, then lower all the way down. Start with 3–4 sets of 8–12 reps, resting 90 seconds, 3 times a week. Level up when you can do 3 sets of 12–15 clean reps.

**Can't do one yet?** Build it in this order: Dead Hang, Scapular Pull-Up, Australian Row, then Negative Pull-Up (jump to the top and lower as slowly as you can).
**Too easy?** Try the L-Sit Pull-Up or the Chest-to-Bar Pull-Up.
Full breakdown: https://brenohildebrand.github.io/calisthenics-comics/m/pull-up/

**Most common mistake:** kipping or swinging to cheat reps. Pause in the dead hang between reps.
